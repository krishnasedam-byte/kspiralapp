Moving shares between your own demat accounts is not a sale and triggers no capital gains. The original purchase date and cost are carried across, so your holding period is preserved and long-term holdings stay long-term.
Import Tradebook
A CSV export of your trades — Zerodha Console → Reports → Tradebook, or the equivalent from ICICI Direct. Columns are detected automatically. Re-importing an overlapping period is safe: only genuinely new rows are added.
Only matters for ambiguous dates like 03-04-2024. ISO dates (2024-03-15) are unaffected.
Update Current Prices
Your broker's daily Holdings / Portfolio export (not the tradebook) — ICICI Direct's Portfolio Equity Summary, or Zerodha Console's Holdings Statement (.xlsx). Both upload directly. Matching is by ISIN, so a broker's own symbol spelling doesn't matter. This only updates current price — average cost and realised P/L still come from the ledger.
